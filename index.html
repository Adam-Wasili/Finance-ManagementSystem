Finance tracker

Know where every kwacha goes.

A small budgeting app that records income and expenses, groups spending by category and keeps a running balance. It began as a Python and SQLite desktop tool and has been rebuilt here for the browser.

  • Add, edit and delete transactions in seconds.
  • See this month's spending broken down by category.
  • Filter by type, category, month or keyword.
  • Your data stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded.

Passwords are salted and hashed before they are stored. This is still a front-end demo, so please do not reuse a real password.

Everything is saved in this browser (localStorage). Clearing your browser data will erase the accounts and transactions.
